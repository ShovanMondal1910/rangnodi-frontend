About Rangnodi

Celebrating the timeless heritage of authentic Jamdani and handcrafted accessories, weaving centuries of tradition into contemporary elegance.

Our Story & Heritage

Rangnodi was born out of a profound passion for Bangladesh's rich textile heritage and indigenous craftsmanship. For centuries, Bangladeshi weavers have spun delicate tales into the threads of Jamdani—a UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.

We bridge the gap between master rural artisans and modern lifestyle connoisseurs. Every product at Rangnodi—from handloomed Jamdani weaves, artisan jewellery, handcrafted bags, wallets, coin pouches, to unique gift items—is crafted with patience, love, and meticulous precision.
